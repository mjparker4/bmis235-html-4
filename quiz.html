Notes Quiz

Upload or paste your notes from any class and this page turns them into a quiz. Everything happens in your browser: your notes aren't sent anywhere, and no AI writes the questions. It builds them from the definitions and sentences in your notes.

1 · Add your notes

Upload .txt, .md, or .html files, or drag them onto the box. For Word, Google Docs, or PDF notes, copy the text and paste it in.

2 · Make the quiz

How to write notes that make good questions
  • Definitions on their own line work best: Term: what it means or Term - what it means. Each one becomes a "which term is this?" or "which one describes this term?" question.
  • Sentences like Equity is ... or Depreciation means ... count as definitions too.
  • Full sentences become fill-in-the-blank questions. A term you defined somewhere else in your notes, or a number, is usually what gets blanked out.
  • Headings (# Chapter 4) and bullet points are fine. Very short fragments get skipped.
  • You need at least 4 definitions or a handful of full sentences for multiple choice to work well.
