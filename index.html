Study Tools

I learn best by testing myself and finding out right away what I got wrong, not by rereading my notes. So I built a few tools that do that for me. Add your notes once and use them for the quiz, the flashcards, and the cheat sheet.

Notes Quiz Upload or paste your notes from any class and get a multiple-choice and fill-in-the-blank quiz. Every answer shows the line from your notes it came from. Flashcards Every definition in your notes becomes a card. Flip it, sort it into "Got it" or "Still learning," and keep going until you know them all. Cheat Sheet Your notes on one printable page, sorted by section, with everything you missed in the quiz or flashcards flagged to review.

No notes handy? Each tool has sample notes on accounting basics you can try them with. Your notes stay in your browser and aren't sent anywhere.
