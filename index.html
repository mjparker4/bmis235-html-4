HTML Study Lab

I learn best by practicing and getting feedback right away, not by rereading notes. So I built this site to study HTML-1 through HTML-3 the way that works for me: try something, see exactly what went wrong, and try again.

Path Trainer Write the paths for links, images, and stylesheets, then fix a broken menu. It shows step by step how the browser follows your path. Notes Quiz Upload or paste your class notes and it turns them into a multiple-choice quiz, right in your browser. Cheat Sheet The main rules from HTML-1 to HTML-3 on one page, for when you get stuck.
