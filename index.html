Head vs. Body

Every web page has two parts. The head holds information about the page that visitors don't see on the page itself, like the title in the browser tab and the link to the stylesheet. The body holds everything visitors do see: headings, paragraphs, images, links, the menu, and the footer.

<!DOCTYPE html>             always the first line
<html lang="en">
<head>                      information about the page (not shown on it)
  <meta charset="utf-8">
  <title>My Page</title>    shows in the browser tab
  <link rel="stylesheet" href="styles.css">
</head>
<body>                      everything visitors see
  <nav>...</nav>
  <h1>Welcome</h1>
  <p>A paragraph.</p>
  <footer>...</footer>
</body>
</html>

I learn best by testing myself and finding out right away what I got wrong, not by rereading notes. So this site teaches head vs. body with three tools that all come loaded with the lesson:

Cheat Sheet The whole lesson on one printable page, with anything you missed in the quiz or flashcards flagged to review.
