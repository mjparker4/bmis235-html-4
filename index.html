Turn Your Notes Into a Quiz

I learn best by testing myself and finding out right away what I got wrong, not by rereading my notes. So I built a tool that does that for me: give it your class notes and it quizzes you on them.

1. Add your notes Upload a .txt, .md, or .html file, or paste notes from Word or Google Docs. Notes from any class work.
2. Take the quiz It turns your definitions and sentences into multiple-choice and fill-in-the-blank questions.
3. See what you missed Every answer shows the line from your notes it came from, and you can retry just the ones you got wrong.

Start a quiz

No notes handy? The quiz page has sample notes from BMIS 235 (HTML‑1 to HTML‑3) you can try it with. Your notes stay in your browser and aren't sent anywhere.
